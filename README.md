# Akusinachi Homes Limited – Real Estate Platform

## Overview

Akusinachi Homes Limited is a real estate platform built to help property buyers, investors, and prospective homeowners discover available properties, make enquiries, and connect directly with the business.

The platform was designed to strengthen the company’s digital presence while providing a structured and user-friendly way to present listings, property details, and customer engagement channels.

## My Role

**Full-Stack Developer**

I handled the end-to-end development of the platform, covering both frontend implementation and backend functionality.

My work focused on building a scalable real estate website that combines property presentation, lead generation, and customer communication in one digital experience.

## Business Problem

The company needed a digital platform that could:

- Present property listings professionally
- Improve online trust and business credibility
- Allow prospective buyers to browse available properties easily
- Create a direct enquiry and consultation pathway
- Support long-term customer acquisition

## Solution Delivered

I designed and developed a full-stack real estate platform that allows users to explore listings, view property details, and contact the company directly.

The platform was structured around three major user journeys:

- Browsing available properties
- Exploring property details
- Making enquiries or scheduling consultations
